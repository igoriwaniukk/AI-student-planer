# Filming guide: "My whole week in Pulgo" (real hand + real app)

The goal is a 6–7 s insert. Your hand holds the phone, the other hand's finger taps the Pulgo icon, the Calendar opens, and you tap through Mon → Sun. Claude edits your raw clip afterwards, so you don't need perfect timing.

## 1. Set up the demo account (once, ~5 min)

1. Create a **separate demo account** in Pulgo. Use the name **Alex**, so none of your own data is on camera.
2. Set the app language to **English** (Settings).
3. Plan to **film on a Monday**, so the whole week (Mon–Sun) is still ahead on the Calendar strip. If you film on another day, move every date below by the same amount.
4. Add these with the **+** button → **Exam or deadline**:

   | Exam | Subject | Date |
   |---|---|---|
   | Biology test | Biology | **This Thursday** |
   | Math quiz | Math | **Next Monday** |
   | Physics test | Physics | **Next Tuesday** |

   Confirm the prep plan Pulgo suggests for each one ("Confirm prep plan").
5. Add these with the **+** button → **Weekly activity**:
   - Soccer training: Mon + Thu, 5:30 PM, 90 min
   - Piano lesson: Wed, 5:30 PM, 45 min
6. Add 2–3 homework to-dos:
   - English essay (Mon)
   - Math worksheet (Wed)
   - History notes (Tue)
7. Plan the days so each one has study sessions ("Plan day"), and leave some free time.
8. Check every day Mon → Sun on the **Calendar** tab. Each day should show something: a study session, an activity or free time. Thursday should show the Biology test.
9. **Close the app while it's on the Calendar tab with Monday selected**, so it can reopen there.
   - If Pulgo opens on Home instead, that's fine: tap the Calendar tab during the take, and Claude will trim it.

Two things to know:
- The real app shows times in its own format (24-hour, e.g. 17:30). That's fine for real footage.
- Make sure the Pulgo **pug icon** is on your home screen. If the iPhone app isn't installed, open the website in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## 2. Phone you're filming (the one in your hand)

- Brightness **maximum**. Turn on **Do Not Disturb**, so no notifications appear.
- Charge it, so no low-battery icon shows.
- Use a clean first home screen with a dark or purple wallpaper. Put the Pulgo icon somewhere easy to tap (the dock or the middle of the screen).
- Wipe the screen. Fingerprints show on camera.

## 3. Camera (the phone or camera that films)

- **Vertical**, on a tripod or propped up so it doesn't move.
- **4K or 1080p, at 60 fps** if possible.
- Frame the **hand + phone filling ~70–80% of the height**, like your reference.
- **Lock focus and exposure on the phone screen** (long-press it on iPhone → AE/AF Lock). Then pull exposure **down a little**, so the app's colours don't blow out to white.
- Turn off the camera's beauty filters and HDR.

## 4. Set and light

- The **purple Video Studio backdrop** goes behind, about 1–2 m away, so it blurs.
- Put a soft light on the hand from the front-left or front-right. **Tilt the phone slightly** until there's no reflection of the light or the window on the screen.
- Only the hand and phone should be in frame. No face needed.

## 5. The take (≈10–15 s raw; Claude cuts it to 6–7 s)

1. Hold the phone still at chest height for **1 s** on the home screen.
2. Tap the **Pulgo icon** with your index finger. Tap, then move the finger away.
3. Wait for the **Calendar** to appear: the week strip with Thursday's orange exam glow.
4. Tap **MON → TUE → WED → THU → FRI → SAT → SUN** on the strip, about **one tap per second**. After each tap, move the finger slightly away, so the day's plan is visible.
5. On Sunday, **hold still for 1–2 s**.

Do **3–5 takes**. Keep the holding hand as steady as you can; a little natural movement is fine and looks real.

## 6. Send it

- Send the **original files**: AirDrop, Google Drive, or "Send as file". Messengers like WhatsApp compress the video and blur the app text.
- Claude then sends back the finished `pulgo-week.mp4`: trimmed, sped up between taps, with punch-in zooms on the week strip, a purple colour grade, 1080×1920, and an SFX track.
